# Product

Inferred from the written brief (no interview was possible); assumptions are marked.

## Register
Product UI (Operate mode): a daily study tool. Scanability, speed and trust outrank expression.

## Users and context
Persian-speaking IELTS candidates, mostly on phones, in short sessions (commute, breaks), often with poor connectivity. Assumption: B1-B2 English, motivated but time-poor.

## Purpose
Retain IELTS vocabulary over time with a five-box Leitner loop and use it in context (reading, listening, writing, text-based speaking). Reviews and saved cards work offline; AI only assists card creation, extraction and feedback and is never required.

## Principles
- The review loop is the product; due reviews come before new words.
- Words are learned in context, not as bare translations.
- Honest by default: no fake AI output, no invented mastery, truthful denominators.
- Calm and non-punitive: missing a day never shames the learner.

## Anti-references
Gamified confetti dashboards, mock-exam platforms, chatbot UIs, generic purple-gradient SaaS look.

## Tone
Warm, plain Persian. Controls name their action; errors name the problem and the way out.
